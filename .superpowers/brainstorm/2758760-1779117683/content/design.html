crt.frag: Effect Stack (top-to-bottom order)

# Effect Knobs Notes
1 Curvature u_curvature (0–1) Barrel distortion. Warps UVs, creates dark bezel beyond the curve radius.
2 Chromatic Aberration u_chromatic (0–0.1) RGB separation proportional to distance from center. Stronger at curved edges.
3 Scanlines u_scanlineStrength (0–1)
u_scanlineWidth (0.25–0.5)		 Horizontal dark bands. Alternating brightness for odd/even rows.
4 Phosphor Mask u_phosphorStrength (0–1)
u_phosphorScale (px)		 RGB triad dots at sub-pixel level, tiled across screen.
5 Phosphor Bloom u_bloomStrength (0–1) Overlays the blurred downscale texture with a soft-light blend. Inspired by overshifted3's glow.
6 Vignette u_vignetteStrength (0–1) Radial darkening from center to corners.
7 Noise u_noiseStrength (0–1) Per-pixel random flicker. Seed-based, time-independent (static noise).

Knob Philosophy

All effect parameters are const float values at the top of crt.frag. Each has a comment showing the range and what "subtle/balanced/heavy" means. To switch between looks, edit the constants and reload niri. No Rust-side changes needed for the initial version.
