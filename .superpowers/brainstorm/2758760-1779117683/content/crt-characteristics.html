What CRT characteristics do you want?

Pick the effects that define your "classic CRT look" — multi-select is on

A

Scanlines

Horizontal dark lines simulating the electron beam scan pattern. The most iconic CRT feature.

B

Screen Curvature

Barrel or pincushion distortion; the dome-like bulge of a CRT tube, strongest at edges.

C

Chromatic Aberration

RGB color fringing at edges from the curved lens. Already present in overshifted3 but could be toned down.

D

Phosphor Bloom / Glow

Soft fuzzy glow around bright areas. Light bleeding through the phosphor layer. Similar to overshifted3's dome lighting but warmer.

E

Phosphor Mask Pattern

Tiny RGB triad dots or vertical aperture grille lines visible up close. The sub-pixel structure of a CRT.

F

Vignette

Darkening toward the corners, simulating the uneven brightness of a CRT tube.

G

Noise / Signal Interference

Subtle analog noise, occasional horizontal flicker, or rolling interference lines.

H

Mask-Driven Refraction (reuse from overshifted3)

Keep the Poisson direction field + chromatic refraction but toned down. Gives glass-like depth to windows.
